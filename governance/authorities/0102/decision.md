## Rationale and activation prerequisites

This is the smallest currently executable, planner-validated scope that exercises
all eight delivered representative cases while preserving every identified gap.
It permits proof of the governance machinery without inventing missing scenarios,
forcing Diagnostic overrides, relabeling historical evidence, or claiming the
47 excluded assignments have passed. The independent review recomputed all
entry mappings, 47 exclusions, and 26 lost obligations from the exact draft.

Before runtime, finish and review the qualification implementation, validate this
same candidate against current inputs, freeze the source provenance and exact
three fresh run IDs in the batch, and record a separate runtime packet. A failed
batch cannot be repaired by filtering or appending attempts. After successful
qualification, final freeze/activation requires a separate exact-digest approval
that binds that batch and its independently verified evidence. The runner cannot
produce that approval. Any proposal/draft digest change invalidates this scope
approval and requires a new reviewed record. Historical artifacts stay immutable.
